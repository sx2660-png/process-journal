Today we completed the 3D printer training in the makerspace. The part of it that mattered for our project is simple: the machine can match a size we decide on.

We tried to build the cube from wood first. The free stock we were allowed to use was very limited, and the boards had already been cut into pieces of different sizes. Before the cube could exist, we had to ask a prior question — which structure those particular offcuts could even support. The wood was choosing the form.

Printing starts from the structure we need. We set the dimensions, and the machine holds them. The cubes in the photo are that result: one form, at a size we chose.
